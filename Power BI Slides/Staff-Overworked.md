# Staff Well-Being Analysis Slides Documentation

## Orange Groove Cafe – Power BI Presentation

This section explains the design and analysis behind the **Staff Well-Being slides**, which focus on workload pressure during peak hours and across academic periods.

The objective of this section is to evaluate whether staff are being overworked and identify high-pressure time periods.

---

# Slide 1: Staff Workload During the Day

<img width="1417" height="801" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/672a83c7-8209-4fe5-858d-6a663fad2fd6" />

## Purpose

This slide analyses hourly workload to determine whether staff experience operational pressure during peak hours.

It focuses on identifying time ranges where workload exceeds comfortable capacity.

---

## Area Line Graph

A line graph was created showing:

* Hour (X-axis)
* Average items per hour (Y-axis)

The area under the line was filled with colour to visually emphasise workload intensity throughout the day.

### Why an Area Chart?

* Makes peaks more visually impactful
* Highlights workload build-up over time
* Clearly shows peak and off-peak periods

---

## Threshold Line (Overwork Indicator)

A red dashed horizontal threshold line was added at:

60 items per hour

This line represents the estimated capacity at which staff begin to feel overworked.

### Purpose of Threshold:

* Clearly identify overload periods
* Visually compare workload vs capacity
* Support operational decision-making

---

## Observed Insight

The graph shows that:

* Workload rises significantly between 11:00 AM and 2:30 PM
* The peak occurs around 12 PM to 1 PM
* During these hours, the average items per hour exceed 60
* This indicates staff are likely operating under pressure

---

## Insight Comment Box

A comment section was added to explain the findings clearly.

Key insights included:

* Peak hours align with lunch time
* Students finishing classes contribute to increased demand
* High foot traffic causes operational strain
* Staff must maintain service quality under pressure

The comment box transforms raw data into actionable insights for management.

# Slide 2: Are They Overwhelmed?

<img width="1420" height="796" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/02063ffd-b12d-43be-8988-ac60b43b68ad" />

## Purpose

This slide expands the analysis by examining workload across:

* Academic semesters
* Holiday periods

It evaluates whether staff pressure is consistent throughout the year or concentrated in specific periods.

---

## KPI Semester Indicators

Large KPI blocks were added to display:

* Month
* Year
* Selected semester

Purpose:

* Clearly indicate which academic period is being analysed
* Improve clarity when switching filters
* Provide context to workload analysis

---

## Hourly Bar Chart (Detailed View)

A bar chart was created showing:

* Hourly items per day
* Comparison against the 60-item threshold

This provides a clearer breakdown of workload intensity per hour.

---

## Conditional Formatting

Conditional formatting was applied to the bars:

* Purple indicates normal workload
* Pink indicates approaching capacity
* Bright pink/red indicates exceeding capacity

When the items per hour exceed the 60 threshold, the bar colour changes to indicate overload.

Purpose:

* Immediately highlight problem hours
* Make high-pressure periods visually clear
* Improve readability of operational risk

---

## Semester vs Holiday Comparison

The slide includes buttons to filter between:

* Oct 22
* Apr 23
* Oct 23
* Holiday period

### Insight

During semesters:

* Significant spikes occur during lunch hours
* Staff are overloaded during peak times

During holidays:

* Sales drop sharply
* Workload falls below capacity
* Staff operate well below pressure levels

This contrast shows:

* Overload during academic terms
* Under-utilisation during holidays

---

## Insight Comment Section

Insight comments summarise findings:

* Staff feel overwhelmed during peak hours (11 AM – 3 PM)
* Orders exceed 60 items per hour during lunch
* Team capacity is stretched
* Holiday periods show significantly lower workload
* Resource allocation may need adjustment

These comments clearly connect data to operational impact.

---

# Key Findings

1. Peak pressure occurs during lunch hours.
2. The 60 items/hour threshold is exceeded consistently during semesters.
3. All locations experience workload spikes during school terms.
4. During semester breaks, workload drops significantly.
5. There is imbalance between peak overload and holiday under-utilisation.

---

# Operational Recommendations

Based on insights:

* Increase staffing during peak hours
* Consider staggered shifts
* Improve process efficiency during lunch rush
* Adjust staffing levels during holiday periods

---

# Conclusion

The Staff Well-Being slides were designed to:

* Identify overload periods
* Compare academic vs holiday performance
* Use conditional formatting to highlight risk
* Provide clear management insights

The combination of threshold indicators, dynamic filters, and explanatory comments transforms workload data into actionable operational strategy.

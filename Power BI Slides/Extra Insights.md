# Slide 2: Menu Price Comparison and Profit Margin

<img width="1428" height="794" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3a6d0ba8-b42e-4dfe-86d9-cbaefb26873c" />

## Purpose

This slide analyses the effect of menu price changes on profit margins and sales.

The goal is to evaluate whether price increases improved profitability.

---

## Profit Margin Comparison Chart

A bar chart was used to compare:

* Old profit margins
* New profit margins

Purpose:

* Show impact of price increase
* Identify items with highest margin growth
* Compare before vs after price adjustment

---

## Table Visual for Detailed Comparison

A table was added showing:

* Menu item
* Old price and cost
* New price and cost
* Profit margins

Purpose:

* Provide detailed breakdown
* Support accurate comparison
* Allow validation of margin changes

---

## Key Insight

After the menu price increase:

* Profit margins increased across items
* Revenue remained stable with slight drop in some items
* Overall profit increased significantly

This shows a positive relationship between price increase and profitability.

---

## Link to Profitability Slide

This slide was linked to the profitability analysis page.

Purpose:

* Check if sales dropped after price increase
* Compare revenue before and after price change
* Validate business impact

Result:
Sales dropped slightly for some items, but overall profit increased due to higher margins.

# Slide 3: Sales Trends Analysis

<img width="1417" height="795" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ac149af2-94c5-4f93-bfb3-513fba999b0b" />

## Purpose

This slide analyses overall sales trends across time.

It focuses on:

* Revenue by hour
* Revenue by month
* Revenue by semester

---

## Revenue by Hour Graph

Shows revenue generated throughout the day.

Insight:

* Highest revenue occurs between 12 PM and 1 PM
* Lowest revenue occurs during opening and closing hours
* Lunch period generates peak sales

---

## Revenue by Month Graph

Shows monthly revenue trends.

Insight:

* Highest revenue months: January and November
* Lowest revenue months: March, June, September
* Revenue drops during holiday periods
* Revenue rises after holidays

---

## Revenue by Semester Graph

Shows performance across semesters.

Insight:

* Revenue increases steadily each semester
* Later semesters generate higher revenue
* Growth may be due to price increase and higher demand

---





# Part 2A: Revenue, Cost and Profit Comparison

## Orange Groove Cafe – Power BI Analysis Slides

This section explains the design and analysis behind the **Part 2A slides**, which compare revenue, total costs, and profit across the three café locations.

Locations analysed:

* Food Club 22
* Makan Place
* Munch

The purpose of these slides is to evaluate financial performance and identify the most profitable outlet.

---

# Revenue Analysis Slide

<img width="1427" height="798" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9400baa0-d7b7-4a7c-8a06-d8cb6d235eb0" />

## Purpose

The revenue slide compares how much revenue each location generates over time and identifies which outlet performs best.

---

## Ribbon Graph (Monthly Comparison)

A ribbon graph was used to show which location generated the highest revenue each month.

Purpose:

* To visually compare performance across locations
* To track which outlet leads in revenue over time
* To observe trends and changes across months

The ribbon format makes it easy to see which location dominates revenue in each period.

---

## Revenue Distribution Pie Chart

A pie chart was added to show the total revenue contribution of each location.

Purpose:

* To represent overall revenue share
* To identify the highest revenue generating outlet
* To compare location contribution to total revenue

### Insight

Makan Place consistently generates the most revenue across all locations.

---

## Semester Pie Chart

A semester pie chart was added to show which semester’s data is currently displayed.

Purpose:

* Helps users understand which academic period is being analysed
* Improves clarity when switching between semesters
* Supports academic calendar-based analysis

---

## Slicers Added

Slicers were included for:

* Month selection
* Semester selection

Purpose:

* Allow filtering of specific time periods
* Enable focused analysis
* Improve interactivity of dashboard

---

## Total Revenue KPI

A KPI card displaying total revenue was added.

Purpose:

* Shows overall revenue generated
* Provides quick summary of performance
* Supports high-level financial overview

---

## Insights Added (Comment Box)

Insight comments were added to explain trends:

* Revenue reached its lowest point during semester breaks
* Revenue increases steadily when school reopens
* Makan Place consistently generates the highest revenue
* Revenue peaks when student activity is highest

These comments help users understand trends without analysing the chart manually.

# Profit/Loss Analysis Slide

<img width="1415" height="794" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73116d4a-5fe8-49a8-a746-7848320b7342" />

## Purpose

This slide compares profit performance across all locations and identifies the most profitable outlet.

---

## Ribbon Graph for Profit

A ribbon graph was used to compare profit across locations monthly.

Purpose:

* Identify most profitable location
* Track profit trends over time
* Detect loss periods

### Key Insight

All three locations experience losses during semester breaks when student activity is low.

This occurs because:

* Sales drop significantly
* Fixed costs remain constant
* Profit becomes negative

---

## Profit Distribution Pie Chart

A pie chart shows total profit contribution by location.

Purpose:

* Identify which outlet contributes most profit
* Compare profit share
* Support strategic decisions

### Insight

Makan Place is consistently the most profitable location.

---

## Profit KPI

A KPI card shows total profit.

Purpose:

* Provide overall profitability summary
* Support quick evaluation of business performance

---

## Most Profitable Location Indicator

Instead of using a slicer, an image of the most profitable location was added.

Purpose:

* Visually highlight top performing outlet
* Make dashboard more engaging
* Provide quick identification of best location

Makan Place was displayed as the most profitable location.

---

## Insights Added (Comment Box)

Insight comments were included to explain trends:

* Makan Place has been the most profitable location over the years
* Profit trends follow revenue trends closely
* Profit surged in the last semester
* Possible reason: price increase and higher demand
* All locations experienced losses during semester breaks due to absence of students

These insights help explain financial performance clearly.

---

# Conclusion

The Part 2A slides were designed to compare revenue, cost, and profit across all locations.

They provide:

* Monthly performance comparison
* Total distribution by location
* Semester-based analysis
* Profitability insights
* Clear business recommendations

Interactive visuals, slicers, and insight comments help users easily understand café performance and support decision-making.

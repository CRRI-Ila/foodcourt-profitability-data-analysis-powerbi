# Cafe Business Profitability and Operations Analysis

Power BI Data Anylitics Project

---

## Project Overview

This project presents a business analytics study of a foodcourts business operating across three food court locations between October 2022 and October 2023. The objective was to transform operational data into meaningful insights that support decision-making related to profitability, operational efficiency, pricing strategy, and staff well-being.

The dashboard was developed using Power BI to analyze financial performance together with operational factors affecting both business outcomes and employee workload. The project demonstrates how data analytics can be applied to real business environments to identify improvement opportunities.

---

## Project Objectives

The analysis aimed to address the following business questions:

* Which food court generates the strongest financial performance?
* What revenue and sales trends are observed over time?
* Are staff members experiencing excessive workload?
* Which food and beverage products contribute most to profitability?
* How did pricing adjustments impact revenue performance?
* Is the Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte a sustainable product financially?
* What operational improvements can enhance overall profitability?

---

## Tools and Skills Demonstrated

* Power BI dashboard development
* Data cleaning and preparation
* Data modeling and relationship design
* DAX measure development
* Business intelligence analysis
* Data visualization and storytelling
* Analytical problem solving

---

## Data Description

The dataset consisted of operational records collected from three food court outlets, including:

* Sales transactions
* Product pricing information (previous and revised prices)
* Ingredient cost data
* Staff transfer and workload records
* Product quantities sold
* Revenue and cost performance metrics

The data reflects real operational business conditions involving financial and workforce variables.

---

## Data Preparation and Cleaning

Before analysis, the dataset required preparation to ensure consistency and reliability. Key steps included:

* Removing duplicate transaction records
* Standardizing date formats
* Cleaning inconsistent product naming
* Managing missing values
* Creating calculated revenue and cost fields
* Structuring datasets for analytical modeling

Data preparation ensured that subsequent analysis produced accurate insights.

---

## Data Modeling

A relational data model was constructed within Power BI linking multiple datasets:

* Sales transactions
* Product information
* Ingredient cost tables
* Staff operational data
* Food court locations

The relationships enabled integrated analysis across financial performance, staffing workload, and product profitability.

---

## DAX Measures Developed

Custom DAX measures were created to evaluate key business performance indicators, including:

* Total revenue
* Total cost
* Profit and profit margin percentage
* Sales quantity metrics
* Staff transfer counts
* Outlet performance comparison
* Product profitability analysis

These calculations converted transactional data into decision-support metrics.

---

## Dashboard Analysis

### Revenue and Sales Trend Analysis

Revenue performance was examined across all three food courts over the two-year period. Time-based analysis identified growth patterns, seasonal variations, and revenue stability.

### Food Court Profitability Analysis

Financial performance was compared between locations to evaluate operational efficiency, cost structures, and contribution to overall profitability.

### Product Performance Analysis

Food and beverage items were evaluated based on both sales volume and profit contribution. The analysis highlighted differences between popularity and financial performance.

### Case Study: Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte

A focused product analysis assessed:

* Sales quantity trends
* Ingredient cost impact
* Profit margin sustainability
* Contribution to total revenue

This case study demonstrated how individual products influence overall business outcomes.

### Pricing Strategy Comparison

Historical pricing was compared against revised pricing structures to evaluate business impact, customer demand response, and revenue stability.

### Staff Well-being Analysis

Operational analytics extended beyond financial measures to evaluate workforce conditions. Staff transfer frequency and workload distribution were analyzed to identify potential overworking risks and staffing imbalances.

---

## Key Insights

* Revenue performance varied across food court locations.
* Sales trends showed identifiable seasonal patterns.
* High-selling products were not always the most profitable due to cost factors.
* Pricing adjustments contributed to improved revenue consistency.
* Some staff members experienced higher operational workload levels.
* Sustainable profitability requires balancing financial performance with workforce well-being.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were proposed:

* Optimize staff scheduling to reduce workload imbalance
* Promote higher-margin products strategically
* Monitor ingredient costs for specialty beverages
* Maintain data-driven pricing adjustments
* Use dashboard monitoring for continuous operational decision-making

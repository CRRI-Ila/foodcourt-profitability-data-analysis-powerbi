

# Matcha Latte Analysis Slides Documentation

## Orange Groove Cafe – Power BI Presentation

This document explains the design and analysis behind the **Matcha Latte analytics slides** used in the presentation.

The slides focus on analysing the performance of the **Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte** across locations, time, and customer behaviour.

---

## Purpose of Slide

This slide provides an overall performance summary of the Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte, including sales, cost, and profitability insights.

It allows users to quickly understand how the drink performs across time and locations.

---

## Visual Design Elements

### Drink Image

A visual image of the Matcha Latte was added to:

* Clearly indicate the product being analysed
* Improve visual storytelling
* Make the dashboard more engaging and product-focused

---

## KPI Cards Added

Several KPI indicators were added to summarise performance:

### Items Sold Per Day

Displays the average number of Matcha Latte drinks sold daily.
This helps identify demand and popularity of the drink.

### Total Quantity Sold

Shows the total number of Matcha Latte drinks sold across the selected period.

### Number of Transactions

Indicates how many transactions included the Matcha Latte.
This helps measure customer purchasing behaviour.

### Total Revenue

Shows the total revenue generated from Matcha Latte sales.

### Total Ingredient Cost

Displays total cost of ingredients used to prepare the drink.
This supports cost and profitability analysis.

---

## Profit vs Cost Donut Chart

A donut-style pie chart was created to display:

* Total cost
* Total profit

Purpose:

* To visually show how much revenue becomes profit
* To quickly identify profit margins
* To support business decision-making

The donut format was chosen to clearly separate cost and profit portions.

---

## Time Filter Buttons for Line Graph

Four buttons were added:

* All
* Month
* Quarter
* Year

These buttons allow users to change the time view of the line graph.

Purpose:

* Control number of data points displayed
* Allow detailed or summary view
* Improve interactivity
* Enable trend analysis across different time levels

The line graph shows number of sales over time and updates based on selected button.

---

## Location Slicer

A slicer was added for location selection.

Purpose:

* Filter data by canteen location
* Compare performance across locations
* Analyse where the drink performs best

Example locations:

* Food Club 22
* Makan Place
* Munch

---

## Location Comparison: Makan Place vs Food Club

A comparison chart was added to analyse:

* Number of drinks sold at Makan Place
* Number of drinks sold at Food Club

### Insight

The Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte is most popular at Makan Place, where it consistently outperforms sales compared to Food Club and other canteens.

However, Food Club shows growing popularity and has potential for increased sales.

---

## Eat-In vs Takeaway Analysis

A chart comparing:

* Eat-in orders
* Takeaway orders

### Insight

Across all locations and semesters, customers show a stronger preference for dining in rather than takeaway when purchasing the Matcha Latte.

This suggests:

* Customers prefer consuming the drink fresh on-site
* Café environment enhances the drinking experience
* Opportunity to improve takeaway packaging if needed

---

## Monthly Trend Comparison

A timeline chart was used to track sales across months.

### Insight

Sales peaked during November and dropped slightly in December, indicating seasonal or academic calendar influence on purchasing behaviour.

# Conclusion

The Matcha Latte analysis slides were designed to:

* Provide clear product-focused insights
* Compare performance across locations
* Evaluate profitability
* Understand customer behaviour
* Support business decision-making

Interactive slicers, buttons, and charts allow users to explore the data dynamically and gain deeper insights into the performance of the Soymilk Pure Matcha Latte.
